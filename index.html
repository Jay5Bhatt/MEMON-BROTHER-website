The House of Memon Brothers

Curated with care.
Worn with confidence.

Memon Brothers was founded on a simple belief — that true luxury lies not in price, but in discernment. From our home in Gujarat, we travel markets, study houses and handle every piece ourselves before it earns a place in our collection.

A timepiece, a fragrance, a frame — each is chosen for its craftsmanship and its character, then offered at an honest price. Nothing enters our shelves that we would not proudly wear ourselves.
